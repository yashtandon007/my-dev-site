About the App

GeoTag Timestamp Camera is a versatile camera app designed for travelers, photographers, and professionals who need to document events with absolute accuracy. Automatically add precise geotags, timestamps, and location details to your captures instantly.

Key Features

Pin Date & Location

Automatically stamps latitude, longitude, and custom notes onto every photo.

No Watermarks

Enjoy clean photos without promotional marks or hidden branding.

GPS Map Integration

View exactly where your memories were captured with built-in map tracking.

Instant Identification

Use location stamps for quick memory recall and professional documentation.

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. GeoTag Timestamp Camera does not upload your personal photos or location data to any external servers. All processing happens locally on your device. We only request permissions (Camera, Location, Storage) necessary for the app to function as described.

For a detailed view of our privacy practices, please contact us or refer to the privacy section within the app settings.
